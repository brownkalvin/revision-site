Rules for using Revision Practice

Last updated 30 September 2026 (version 3)

The short version

  • You must be 13 or older to have an account.
  • Be kind. No bullying, hate, sexual content, threats, or sharing anyone's personal details.
  • If something upsets you or seems wrong, use Report or Block. Reports go to us, not to the other person.
  • If you break the rules we may remove what you posted, turn off your friends, groups and sharing, or delete your account.
  • The questions are here to help you revise, but they might contain mistakes. Check with your teacher if you're unsure.

1. About these rules

Revision Practice (kelvincard.co.uk) is run by Kelvin Brown. By making an account you agree to these rules. Please also read our privacy notice. If you're under 18, it's a good idea to talk to a parent or carer about using the site, especially the friends and groups features.

2. Your account

  • You must be 13 or older. If we find out someone under 13 has an account, we'll delete it.
  • Only make one account, and don't share your password.
  • Pick a username that's kind and doesn't include your real name, school or other personal details. We may change or remove usernames that break these rules.

3. Community rules

These apply to usernames, group names, deck names, questions you write, and anything you share. Don't:

  • bully, harass, embarrass or threaten anyone, or encourage others to;
  • post anything hateful about someone's race, religion, disability, sex, sexuality, gender identity or other characteristic;
  • post or ask for sexual content, or anything violent or shocking;
  • encourage self-harm, suicide, eating disorders or dangerous challenges;
  • share personal information (addresses, phone numbers, social media accounts, photos, school names) about yourself or anyone else;
  • ask to meet up with people you only know from the site;
  • post anything illegal, including copyrighted material you don't have permission to share;
  • spam, scam, pretend to be someone else, or try to break, hack or cheat the site (including the coins).

4. How we keep people safe

  • Reporting. Every shared deck and every user has a Report button. You can also email kelvinleebrown08@gmail.com. Reports are private.
  • Blocking. Blocking someone stops them sending you decks, friend requests or group invites.
  • Private by default. There's no chat. People can only find you by typing your exact username, and only friends can send you decks or invite you to groups.
  • What we do with reports. We aim to check reports within 48 hours, and sooner if someone might be at risk. We remove content that breaks these rules or the law as quickly as we can.
  • Illegal content. We'll remove it, and where the law requires or someone is at risk, we'll report it to the police or the National Crime Agency.
  • Consequences. Depending on how serious it is, we may remove content, turn off someone's friends, groups and sharing, or delete their account.

5. Complaints and appeals

If you think we got a decision wrong (for example, we removed something of yours, turned off your sharing, or didn't act on your report), or you want to complain about how we're keeping the site safe, email kelvinleebrown08@gmail.com with your username and what happened. We'll look at it again and reply within 14 days.

6. Coins and themes

Coins are just for fun. They have no money value, can't be bought, sold or swapped, and don't carry over anywhere else. We may correct coins earned by cheating or through a bug, and we may change prices or themes.

7. Adverts, sponsors and donations

To help cover running costs, the home and subject pages may show a small advert from Google, labelled "Advertisement", and one sponsor, labelled "Sponsored". Adverts are never shown while you're practising. Adverts are not personalised, and we block adverts for gambling, alcohol, dating and similar things. We don't check or endorse the products advertised. If you see an advert that seems wrong for a revision site, please email kelvinleebrown08@gmail.com and tell us what it was.

Donations are optional and meant for parents, carers and teachers. They are made on a separate website under its own terms, and don't give extra features or change anything about your account.

8. Things you create

You own the questions you write. By sharing something with friends or a group, you let us store it and show it to the people you shared it with. Only share questions you've written yourself or have permission to share.

9. The questions and answers

We work hard to get the questions and solutions right, but mistakes can happen. Use the site to help your revision, not instead of your teacher or official exam board materials. If you spot a mistake, please use Report on the question.

10. Changes and availability

We may update the site or these rules. If we make important changes to the rules, we'll ask you to agree again. We try to keep the site running, but can't promise it will always be available.

11. The law

These rules are covered by the law of England and Wales. Nothing in them affects your legal rights.

Getting help

If you or someone else is in danger, call 999.

Childline: free and confidential, any time. Call 0800 1111 or visit childline.org.uk.

CEOP: if someone online is making you feel unsafe, sending you sexual messages or asking to meet: ceop.police.uk.

Samaritans: call 116 123, free, any time.
