Privacy notice

Last updated 30 September 2026 (version 3)

The short version

  • We only collect what we need to run the site: your email, the username you pick, your practice progress, and any decks you share with other people.
  • Other people only ever see your username. Never your email. There's no chat or messaging on the site.
  • There may be a small advert at the bottom of the home and subject pages. Adverts are never personalised to you, and advertising cookies are only used if you say yes. We never sell your data and never use your location.
  • You can download everything we have about you, or delete your account, from the Account page whenever you like.
  • If you're worried about anything, tell a parent, carer or teacher, or email us at kelvincardsupport@gmail.com.

Who we are

kelvincard (kelvincard.co.uk) is run by Kelvin Brown. We are the "controller" of your personal information, which means we decide how it's used and we're responsible for looking after it. You can contact us at kelvincardsupport@gmail.com.

Who can use the site

You need to be 13 or older to make an account. Anyone can practise without an account; in that case your progress stays on your own device and we don't receive it.

Our legal reasons (lawful bases)

Data protection law says we need a lawful reason to use personal information. Ours are:

  • Legitimate interests: providing the free revision service you've chosen to use, including accounts, progress, coins, friends, groups and deck sharing. We've checked this is fair to you, and we don't use your information in ways you wouldn't expect.
  • Legitimate interests and legal obligations: keeping people safe. The Online Safety Act requires us to deal with reports and remove illegal content. If we find content showing child sexual abuse, the law requires us to report it to the National Crime Agency, and we may share information with the police where someone is at risk.

We only ask for adverts that are not personalised, so they aren't chosen using a profile of you, and we don't give advertisers your account details, progress or username. We don't make automated decisions that have legal or similarly significant effects on you.

Companies that help us run the site

These companies process information for us, only on our instructions:

  • Supabase: stores accounts and saved data on servers in London, UK.
  • GitHub (GitHub Pages) and Netlify: host the website files.
  • Resend: sends account emails such as confirmation and password reset emails.
  • Google (AdSense): shows the adverts and the cookie choices message. Google decides how it uses the information it collects for advertising, as a separate "controller"; see how Google uses information from sites that use its services. We ask Google for non-personalised adverts only, and we block adverts for things like gambling, alcohol and dating.
  • jsDelivr and cdnjs (Cloudflare): deliver some of the code the site uses and the maths display. They see your IP address when your browser downloads these files.

Some of these companies are based in, or use servers in, the USA. When information leaves the UK, it's protected by the UK's data bridge with the USA or by contract terms approved under UK law.

Information stored on your device

The site stores information in your browser (called "local storage") so you stay logged in and your progress, settings and chosen theme work, even offline. This is essential for the site to work, so we don't need to ask for consent. Logging out removes your saved data from that device.

Advertising cookies. When adverts are shown, Google's cookie message asks whether it may use cookies and similar technology for advertising (for example to limit how often you see the same advert, and to detect fraud). This is your choice, and saying no doesn't stop you using the site. You can change your mind at any time with Cookie choices at the bottom of the page. We don't use any analytics.

Sponsors and donations

The home page may show one sponsor, marked "Sponsored". It's just a link: we don't share any of your information with sponsors, and if you click it you'll be on their website, under their privacy policy.

Parents and teachers can choose to support the site through a separate donation website. Payments happen entirely on that website; we never see card details. Supporting the site is optional and doesn't change anything about your account.

How long we keep it

  • Your account, progress, coins, friends and groups: until you delete your account.
  • Decks sent straight to a friend: deleted automatically after 12 months. Decks shared in a group: until the person who shared it, the group owner or the site owner removes it, or the group is deleted. (Copies people have saved to their own decks stay with them.)
  • Reports: kept for 12 months after we've dealt with them, so we can spot repeated problems.
  • Deleting your account removes your information straight away, apart from copies in reports (kept as above) and short-lived backups and logs kept by the companies above.
  • If you don't log in for 2 years, we may delete your account.

Your rights

You have the right to:

  • get a copy of your information (use Download my data on the Account page, or email us);
  • correct information that's wrong;
  • delete your information (use Delete my account, or email us);
  • object to how we use it, or ask us to limit how we use it;
  • take your information to another service (the download is in a standard format).

These rights belong to you, whatever your age. A parent or carer can also contact us on your behalf. We'll reply within one month. It's free.

Keeping your information safe

Everything is sent over encrypted connections. Our database only lets each person see their own information and what others have chosen to share with them. If something goes wrong that puts your information at risk, we'll deal with it quickly, tell the Information Commissioner's Office when the law requires it, and tell you if you're affected.

Complaints

If you're unhappy with how we've used your information, please contact us first at kelvincardsupport@gmail.com. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO), the UK's data protection regulator: ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint or 0303 123 1113.

Changes

If we change this notice in an important way, we'll tell you on the site and ask you to read it again.
